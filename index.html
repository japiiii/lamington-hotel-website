Everything a stay in Popondetta needs

Whether you are travelling for business, visiting family, or exploring Oro Province, the essentials are taken care of.

Comfortable rooms
Twin shares, deluxe queen twins, and suites — all air-conditioned with fresh linen and a quiet night's sleep guaranteed.
Paia's Restaurant
A full-service restaurant serving local and international dishes from breakfast through dinner, daily.
Saloon bar & pokies
Wind down at our saloon bar with cold drinks and gaming machines for in-house guests. Strictly 18+.
Conference facilities
A 150-seat conference room with catering packages — ideal for workshops, trainings, and functions.
Guest services
Laundry service, airport and town shuttle, secure parking, and a friendly front desk around the clock.

Laundry & vehicle services

Little conveniences that make a stay easy — just ask at the front desk and we'll arrange it, charged straight to your room.

Laundry service

Same-day washing, drying and pressing with per-item pricing. Drop your laundry at the front desk before 9am and it's back the same evening.

Airport & town shuttle

Scheduled pick-ups and drop-offs for Girua Airport and Popondetta town. Book your shuttle at check-in or the day before travel.

Charters & day trips

Need a vehicle for meetings, site visits, or a run out to the province? Talk to the front desk about charter transport arrangements.

Conference Room

Seats up to 150 guests, with optional catering packages from Paia's Restaurant — a ready venue for workshops, trainings and functions. K600 per day.
