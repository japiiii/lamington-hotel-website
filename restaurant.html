Saloon Bar & Gaming Lounge

When the kitchen winds down, the saloon bar keeps going — cold SP, spirits and soft drinks, sport on the screen, and a gaming lounge with pokies for in-house guests. Entry is strictly 18 and over, and our staff are happy to help you set your own limits — please play responsibly.
